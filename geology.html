01DECEMBER 2019 · RAJASTHAN, INDIA

Ajeetgarh

Mapped the Precambrian geology of Ajeetgarh and adjoining areas, preparing geological maps and studying cross-bedding in the basal Alwar Group. Foliation and lineation helped reconstruct the sequence of deformation.

Mapping · Structural geology
02SEPTEMBER 2018 · UTTARAKHAND, INDIA

Dehradun–Mussoorie

Studied structural and sedimentological features along a dry riverbed, mapped the Himalayan Frontal Thrust and Main Boundary Thrust, and examined the Ganga–Yamuna drainage divide while practicing geological mapping.

Himalayan structures · Sedimentology
03MARCH 2018 · UTTAR PRADESH, INDIA

Varanasi & the Vindhyan region

Collected rocks from the Semri and Rewa formations of the Vindhyan Supergroup, studied sedimentary structures, and measured stromatolites in the Sulkan Valley. The map marks the broad Varanasi field-trip region.

Stratigraphy · Stromatolites
04DECEMBER 2016 · UTTARAKHAND, INDIA

Nainital

Investigated the geological setting of Nainital Lake and the structural and sedimentological features of the Krol and Tal formations—an early introduction to reading the Himalayan rock record in the field.

Field geology · Krol & Tal formations
05SEPTEMBER 2023 · ARIZONA, USA

Meteor Crater, near Flagstaff

Examined the overturned strata at Meteor Crater and the surrounding geology during the Arizona “Holey Tour.” This field trip connected my geological training to the physical record of a meteorite impact.

Impact geology · Overturned strata
06SEPTEMBER 2023 · SAME ARIZONA TRIP

Sunset Crater

Studied the volcanic landscape and ʻaʻā lava flows around Sunset Crater, comparing volcanic landforms with the impact structures visited on the same trip.

The volcano erupted around 1085 CE. Geological context · National Park Service ↗

Volcanology · Lava textures
